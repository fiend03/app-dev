
My Favorite Movie: The Hobbit

Introduction

My favorite movie is **The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey**. It is a *fantasy adventure movie* directed by **Peter Jackson** and based on the novel by **J.R.R. Tolkien**.

I love this movie because it shows the importance of *courage, friendship, teamwork, and determination*. It also takes viewers into a magical world filled with exciting adventures and unforgettable characters.

Story Summary

The story follows **Bilbo Baggins**, a peaceful hobbit who enjoys his comfortable life in the Shire.

One day, the wizard *Gandalf* invites him to join a group of thirteen dwarves led by **Thorin Oakenshield**.

Their mission is to reclaim the **Lonely Mountain** and its treasure from the dragon **Smaug**.

Throughout their journey, they encounter dangerous creatures, unexpected challenges, and mysterious places.

Bilbo discovers that even an ordinary hobbit can become a courageous hero.
